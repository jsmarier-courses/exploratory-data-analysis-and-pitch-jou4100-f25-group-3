## Template for the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Pitch

We sourced the data for this project from the City of Ottawa’s 2021 Long-Form Census, which was originally published in 2023 and provides demographic data regarding Ottawans’ income, ethnicity, nationality, spoken languages, education, employment and other characteristics.

 Our process and methodology followed the structure of the assignment; that is, getting the data and making initial observations about it, conducting a VIMO analysis of the dataset, cleaning and refining the data, visualizing the data for analysis and brainstorming a potential story idea from the data.
 
  The main observation we drew from our data was focused on the subset of the broader census about commuting methods, durations and times for Ottawa’s labour force. Specifically, we observed that over three quarters of the labour force used personal vehicles to commute, inspiring a story idea about what the City of Ottawa is doing to improve its public transit and active transportation networks to incentivize alternative commuting methods.
